The owner's answers of the same day: URL and repository **commercialdistrict** (so the product reads “Commercial District”, the family's convention of a name per path), publish the whole family set, and keep the registry's internal working fields out of the public build (“ตัดออก”: field questions, source ids, merge notes, outline policy versions). “ลักษณะเดียวกับ demo web อื่นๆ” = the CityMETER 3D demo family as CityMETER · University carries it (code base montri-th/university @ed2f350, citymeter-shell 0.6.1), so every family rule that is not about one dataset applies here as written.

- Apply current LDS 0.9.7 (v0.9.7-owner.1, standalone-0.9.7-r1, color-srgb-10). This product uses two base families only: `density.area` (records per km² of a neighbourhood, the map under a dataset) and `count`. Colours come only from `assets/theme.css`, `js/scales.js` and `js/cues.js`; never approximate a value by eye. Analytical colours are the same HEX on both themes; EVID-05 treatments use the `dataState` tokens. Colour scales always use the full 41 LDS samples (owner standing rule “Color scale ขอให้ใช้ 41 scales ตาม LDS เสมอ”). `npm run check:lds -- --lds <Landometer-Design-System-v0.9.7.md>` compares every shipped value with the source file.
- Read `PRODUCT_STATEMENT.md` before changing what the first screen argues. The question is “ย่านไหนเด่นเรื่องอะไร และอะไรมาด้วยกัน”.
- One grain per dataset (`DATA_METHOD.md`): a neighbourhood holds, per dataset, the dataset's located records inside its outline, in the dataset's own unit. Never add counts of two datasets, never show a total of “POIs”. A dataset that has no located record in the neighbourhood's province is out of scope there (no key in `rows[].c`, “ไม่มีข้อมูลในจังหวัดนี้”, “—”), never 0.
- Every comparison names its peers with their number (`DATA_METHOD.md` §5): share of the dataset's located records nationwide, count rank among the neighbourhoods of the provinces the dataset reaches, density rank among those holding at least 3, or one of the few that hold any. Never call a count footfall, sales, revenue, demand or a market share.
- “มาด้วยกัน” is Spearman's ρ of density across neighbourhoods (a correlation, never a cause); roles are the registry's proposals read against this demo's dataset mapping (an inference); “ส่วนผสมคล้ายกัน” is the centred cosine of log density (an inference). The page says so in titles and in `DATA_METHOD.md`, never as a caveat line on screen.
- Outlines are the registry's closed draft outlines, drawn as delivered (display copy simplified with shared edges kept); never edit, merge or redraw one in this repository, never call one a legal or administrative boundary. The 27 codes without a closed outline are searchable, never counted or drawn.
- Aha first: on the whole country, the dataset most concentrated in the neighbourhoods (its share with numerator and denominator) against the neighbourhoods' share of the area, then the next three; in a province, the same within the province; under a dataset, its share and the densest neighbourhood. Snapshot numbers in docs are fixtures, never UI constants.
- Map readings (`js/app.js#computeFills`, `js/layers.js`): highlights — the pillar series of the first highlight with its LDS shape (Land 4 diamond, Location 7 hexagon, Living 1 circle; a ring for none); a dataset — `density.area` by rank among the neighbourhoods in scope. No opacity on a data colour; outside the scope a neighbourhood is a quiet outline and a small hollow pin; from zoom 14 the fill gives way to the same colour on the outline. The open neighbourhood shows its records and its buildings in 3D with the surroundings (`js/regions.js`).
- Records of the open neighbourhood (0.1.3, `js/records.js`, `DATA_METHOD.md` §9a; owner 2026-11-16 “เลือกดูทีละชุด” after “icon ใน symbol ที่ทำอยู่ไม่ช่วยเท่าไรนะครับ”): pins stay the pillar's colour and shape — never a glyph inside a pin. The dataset bar on the map picks one set (`f.show`; reading a dataset with `m` picks it too): its pins larger with names beside them (HTML labels that never cover a pin of the set), every other record a small neutral dot that still answers the pointer. Pointer → the names; click → the card (figure under the dataset's own exact name, the record in its own demo at `?e=<kind>:<key>`, Google Maps, Street View, dataset · source · date · boundary); a phone docks the card under the bar. Never a count across datasets in a tip, list or card. Condominium points (registry dataset `condo`, group Land) are display only: drawn round the open neighbourhood when chosen, dashed, never counted in a neighbourhood, a share, a rank or a role (the dataset's own spatial policy).
